- 2026-10-13 — **Bets settle early; commish can see everyone's** (Claude; Jayson after week 1 of real use: "when do the bets settle? if the game or player has already finished then it needs to be settled then").
  - **Why Wednesday existed:** Sleeper applies stat corrections into Tuesday and those move fantasy points, so settling Sunday can be wrong by Tuesday. Rather than drop the safeguard, settlement now happens twice.
  - `settleReady(season, week)` in `server/book/book.js`, called from `tick()` every 10 minutes for any unsettled week. Grades a **prop** as soon as that player's own NFL game is `post`; a **matchup line** once `isStarted(line)` and every starter on both rosters is `post` or a true bye and Sleeper has both scores; **specials** once every game in the league is done. It does NOT set `book_weeks.settled_at`.
  - **The trap:** `weekState` labels a starter `'bye'` whenever it can't match him to an NFL game, so an ESPN outage looks like a finished lineup. `gamesOver()` therefore requires at least one starter to be genuinely `post`, and `settleReady` bails if ESPN returned fewer than 2 games. Without the `isStarted` guard the whole thing graded lines before kickoff — that's what took the book suite from 44 to 13.
  - `settleWeek` is now the **correction pass**: it selects every line (not just open ones) and re-grades every leg (not just open ones) from the corrected numbers. A leg that changed re-opens its bet through `reopenBet()`, which writes a negative `correction` row to the ledger for whatever was paid, then `gradeOne` settles it again. Logged with a `corrected` count.
  - `betBook()` + `GET /api/book/admin/bets` (commish only): every bet, filterable by week/manager/status, per-manager totals and the house line. Client: `BKB`, `bkLoad()`, `bkBetsBody()`, a `bets` entry in Book controls, CSS prefix `bk-`.
  - Tests: new `scratchpad/booktest/early.mjs`, 12 checks — nothing settles mid-game, both bets graded Sunday night, the week stays open, a second pass pays nothing extra, then a stat correction flips a paid bet and the ledger and balance come out exact. **Note:** `run.mjs` never resets its own fixtures and inherits whatever the previous suite left, so `early.mjs` hands `db.json`/`espn.json` back byte for byte at the end.
