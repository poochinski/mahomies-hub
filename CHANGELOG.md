# Changelog

Newest on top. One line per push.

- 2026-10-11 — **This week briefs**: every game now gets a one-line read on where it stands — who needs how much from how many players and what they're projected for, "Down to the wire", "All but over", "Comeback on", or the final margin — plus tags. Every upset is tagged now, not just the single biggest one, and close games get a Nail-biter tag. (Claude)
- 2026-10-11 — **Play by play fixed**: it said "No plays for him yet" for everyone because Sleeper doesn't give an ESPN id for many players, so nothing matched. The app now works out each player's ESPN id from the game's plays (the athlete who's in the plays that name him), so the play-by-play fills in for live and finished games. (Claude)
- 2026-10-11 — **Play by play + score breakdown**: in Slot vs slot, tap a player's points for his Score Breakdown (every scoring stat × what our league pays for it, with the total). Tap anywhere else on the row (not the name) for his week, which now ends with a Sleeper-style play-by-play: every snap he was part of with down & distance, quarter and clock, the score after the play, what he did, the points it was worth, and his stat line at that moment. Touchdowns are highlighted. It covers the whole game, even plays from before you opened the app. The old "Before tracking" scoring log is gone. (Claude)
- 2026-10-11 — **Slot vs slot gets the play-by-play**: every row now shows the quarter and clock for players in a game ("Q3 8:12 · on pace 14.4") and their stat line so far. Tap a name for the player card; tap anywhere else on the row for his week: points, on-pace, projection, snap share, the full box score, his NFL game score, and a scoring timeline — each catch, carry or kick with the game clock and the points it was worth. The server logs that timeline every 10 seconds during games, starting with this deploy. (Claude)
- 2026-10-11 — **Game-day live speed**: while any NFL game is on, the app now pulls fresh points, stat lines and game clocks from Sleeper every ~10 seconds (was 45–60 s on the server plus another 60 s on the phone, so up to ~2 minutes behind). Phones check in every 10 seconds during games and every minute otherwise, and switching back to Mahomie's Hub from the Sleeper app refreshes immediately. The Updated stamp on Slot vs slot and the Game Center shows seconds and the current pace. (Claude)
- 2026-10-11 — **Home desk fixes**: Slot vs slot no longer shows a stale pregame projection next to a player who's already playing — live players say LIVE and where they're on pace to finish, players who haven't kicked off show their kickoff time and projection, finished players say Final, and the sheet now says when it last updated and refreshes itself every minute while it's open. The home slot chart gives each position its own tile with the two scores stacked, so the numbers stop running together. Last 4 weeks now shows W/L, the score, who they scored against, and how far above or below their pregame projection they finished. (Claude)
